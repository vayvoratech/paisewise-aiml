Risk tolerance describes the level of investment uncertainty or loss that an investor is willing and able to accept. It is influenced by financial circumstances, investment objectives, time horizon and personal comfort with fluctuations. Two people with similar incomes may have different risk tolerances because their financial responsibilities and goals differ. Risk tolerance should not be confused with the ability to take risk. A person may be comfortable with market fluctuations but still have a short investment horizon that limits the amount of risk appropriate for a particular goal. Understanding risk tolerance is an important part of building a suitable financial plan.
