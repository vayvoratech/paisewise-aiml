Financial goals are specific objectives that require money to achieve. Examples include creating an emergency fund, purchasing a home, funding education or planning for retirement. Goals can be classified as short-term, medium-term or long-term based on the time available to achieve them. A useful financial goal should specify the required amount and target date. Inflation should also be considered when estimating future expenses. Once a goal is defined, a person can determine how much needs to be saved or invested regularly. Goal-based financial planning helps connect everyday financial decisions with future requirements and makes it easier to track progress over time.
