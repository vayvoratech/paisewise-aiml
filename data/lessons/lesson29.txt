Insurance is a financial arrangement designed to provide protection against specified risks in exchange for a premium. Life insurance can provide financial protection related to the insured person's life, while health insurance can provide coverage for eligible medical expenses according to policy terms. Insurance is primarily about protection rather than guaranteed investment returns. Important policy characteristics include coverage, exclusions, premium, policy term, waiting periods, deductibles and claim conditions. Understanding these terms helps individuals evaluate the purpose of insurance and distinguish protection products from investment products.
