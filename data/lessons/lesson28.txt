Retirement planning involves preparing financially for future expenses after regular employment income reduces or stops. A retirement plan may consider current age, expected retirement age, current savings, future expenses, inflation and expected investment returns. Because retirement may be a long-term goal, the effects of inflation and compounding can be significant. Regularly reviewing progress can help identify whether savings and investments remain aligned with the goal. Retirement planning should account for uncertainty because future expenses, inflation, income and investment returns may differ from expectations.
