Financial products can involve different types of costs and charges. Mutual funds may have expenses associated with managing and operating schemes. Securities transactions can involve brokerage, taxes, exchange-related charges and other applicable costs. Insurance products involve premiums and may contain additional charges depending on the product. Costs can affect the net return received by an investor. Therefore, understanding the applicable charges before using a financial product is important. Investors should read official product documents and fee information rather than relying solely on promotional material.
