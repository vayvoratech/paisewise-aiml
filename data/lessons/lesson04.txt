An emergency fund is money kept aside for unexpected financial situations. Examples include sudden medical expenses, temporary loss of income, urgent repairs or other unforeseen costs. The main purpose of an emergency fund is to provide liquidity when money is needed quickly. Without an emergency reserve, a person may need to depend on expensive borrowing or sell investments at an inconvenient time. Emergency savings should generally be kept in relatively accessible and low-risk forms. The required amount depends on factors such as income stability, regular expenses, existing obligations and personal circumstances. An emergency fund is different from money intended for long-term investment goals because its primary purpose is financial preparednes
