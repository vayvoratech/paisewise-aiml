Market capitalization represents the total market value of a company's outstanding shares. It is generally calculated by multiplying the current market price per share by the number of outstanding shares. Companies are often grouped into large-cap, mid-cap and small-cap segments according to applicable classification frameworks. Market capitalization can change as share prices change. It provides information about the size of a company in market-value terms but does not by itself indicate whether a company is financially strong or whether its shares are appropriately valued. Investors should consider multiple financial and business factors.
