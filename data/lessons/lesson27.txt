Investment performance describes how an investment has performed over a particular period. Common measures include absolute return, annualized return and benchmark comparison. Past performance can provide useful historical information but does not guarantee future results. Performance should be considered alongside risk, volatility, costs and the period being measured. For mutual funds, investors may compare performance with the relevant benchmark and category while considering the scheme's objective. A strong return accompanied by substantially higher risk may have a different meaning from a similar return achieved with lower volatility.
