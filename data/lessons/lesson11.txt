Asset allocation refers to how money is distributed among different asset classes such as equity, debt, gold and cash. The allocation of a portfolio can influence its overall risk and return characteristics. A portfolio with a higher proportion of volatile assets may experience larger fluctuations, while a portfolio with more conservative assets may have different growth potential. Asset allocation should be connected to financial goals and time horizon. As financial circumstances change, an investor may review the allocation of the portfolio. Understanding asset allocation helps distinguish between owning several investments and actually spreading risk across different asset classes.
