Risk and return are important concepts in investing. Return represents the gain or loss generated by an investment, while risk represents uncertainty around that outcome. Different financial assets have different risk and return characteristics. Equity investments may experience significant price fluctuations, while some fixed-income instruments may have different risks related to interest rates or credit quality. Higher potential returns generally involve accepting some level of uncertainty. Investors should understand that historical returns do not guarantee future performance. Risk should be considered alongside financial goals, time horizon and ability to absorb losses. Understanding risk and return helps people evaluate investments more carefully rather than focusing only on expected returns.
