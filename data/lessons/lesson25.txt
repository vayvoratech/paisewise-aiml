KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is a process used to verify customer identity and required information according to applicable regulations. Financial services may require identity and address-related information before providing certain services. Investors should use official and regulated channels when opening financial accounts or making transactions. Personal information, passwords, PINs and authentication details should be protected. Investors should also be cautious of unsolicited investment offers, guaranteed-return claims and requests for confidential information. Understanding KYC and basic security practices helps investors participate in financial services more safely.
