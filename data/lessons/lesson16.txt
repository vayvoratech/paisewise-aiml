The expense ratio represents the expenses charged for managing and operating a mutual fund scheme, expressed as a percentage of the scheme's assets according to applicable regulations. Expenses can include management-related costs and other permitted operating expenses. Because expenses reduce the assets available to investors, they can affect long-term returns. Comparing expense ratios can therefore be useful when evaluating similar investment products. However, expense ratio should not be considered in isolation. Investment objective, portfolio composition, risk, performance and suitability for the investor's goal are also important factors.
