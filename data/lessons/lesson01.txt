Personal finance is the process of managing money to meet present needs and future goals. It includes earning income, controlling expenses, saving money, investing, managing debt, insurance and financial planning. A good financial plan starts with understanding how much money comes in and where it goes. Tracking income and expenses helps identify unnecessary spending and opportunities to save. Financial goals should be specific and connected to a time period, such as building an emergency fund, paying for education or preparing for retirement. Financial decisions should also consider risk, liquidity and time horizon. Developing financial discipline early can help individuals make informed decisions and avoid unnecessary financial stress.
