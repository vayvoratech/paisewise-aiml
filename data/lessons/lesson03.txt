A budget is a plan for managing income and expenses during a particular period. A simple budget starts by listing all sources of income and regular expenses. Expenses can be divided into essential expenses and discretionary expenses. Essential expenses may include housing, food, utilities and required payments. Discretionary expenses are expenses that may be reduced or postponed. Comparing income with expenses helps identify whether there is a surplus or deficit. A surplus can potentially be allocated toward savings, financial goals or investments. Regularly reviewing a budget helps people understand spending patterns and improve financial discipline. A budget should be realistic and flexible enough to accommodate changing circumstances
