Financial awareness means understanding important financial concepts, products, risks, costs, rights and responsibilities. A financially aware person can distinguish saving from investing, understand basic investment risks, compare financial products and recognize misleading claims. Good financial decisions involve reviewing reliable information, understanding product documents and considering personal financial goals. No single financial product is suitable for every situation. Investors should avoid decisions based solely on social media trends, guaranteed-return promises or pressure from others. Continuous financial education helps people make more informed decisions and develop responsible financial habits.
