The primary market is where new securities are issued to investors. An Initial Public Offering, or IPO, is a process through which a company offers shares to the public. An IPO allows a company to raise capital while giving eligible investors an opportunity to participate in the offering. The offer document contains important information about the company, the issue and associated risks. An IPO should not be viewed as a guaranteed opportunity for profit because the market price after listing can move in either direction. Understanding the company, issue structure, valuation and risks is important when studying public offerings.
