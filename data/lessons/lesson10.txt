Diversification means spreading investments across different assets, securities, sectors or categories rather than concentrating money in one area. The purpose is to reduce concentration risk. For example, a portfolio invested across different sectors may be less dependent on the performance of one particular sector. Diversification does not eliminate investment risk or guarantee profits. Different assets can sometimes decline at the same time. The benefits of diversification depend on how the investments behave relative to one another. A diversified portfolio should therefore be considered in terms of asset classes, sectors, market capitalization and other relevant characteristics.
