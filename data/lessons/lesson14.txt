A Systematic Investment Plan, or SIP, is a facility through which an investor invests a predetermined amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals. The amount may be invested monthly or according to another available frequency. SIPs provide a systematic approach to investing rather than requiring a person to make a separate investment decision each time. When market prices vary, a fixed investment amount may purchase different numbers of units at different times. SIP does not guarantee profits or eliminate market risk. Its main purpose is to provide a disciplined method for making regular investments.
