Equity represents ownership in a company. When an investor purchases shares of a company, they acquire an ownership interest represented by those shares. Equity prices can fluctuate because of company performance, economic conditions, interest rates, investor expectations and market sentiment. Equity investments can provide returns through price appreciation and, where applicable, dividends. However, returns are not guaranteed and losses are possible. Understanding company fundamentals, valuation, business risks and market conditions is important when studying equity investments. Diversification can also help reduce exposure to the performance of a single company.
