A mutual fund pools money from multiple investors and invests the collected money in securities according to the scheme's investment objective. Investors receive units representing their participation in the scheme. Mutual funds are managed by professional fund managers and operate according to regulatory requirements and scheme documents. Different mutual fund schemes can have different objectives, asset classes, risks and costs. Examples include equity funds, debt funds and hybrid funds. Mutual funds can provide diversification because a single scheme may hold multiple securities. However, mutual fund investments are subject to market and other applicable risks, and returns are not guaranteed.
