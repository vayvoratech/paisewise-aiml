A bond is a debt instrument through which an issuer raises money from investors. The issuer generally agrees to make payments according to specified terms and repay the principal according to the instrument's conditions. Government securities are debt instruments issued by governments. Bonds may differ in maturity, interest structure, credit quality and liquidity. Bond prices can change before maturity because of market conditions, especially changes in interest rates and perceptions of credit risk. Understanding the relationship between bond prices, yields, maturity and interest rates is important when studying fixed-income investments.
