FEES AND EXPENSES
Development RAG Knowledge Source

Investment products can have costs and expenses. Recurring costs can affect long-term outcomes because money paid in fees is not available for investment.

An expense ratio commonly expresses annual fund operating expenses as a percentage of fund assets.

Funds may charge management fees and other operating expenses. Buying and selling securities can involve transaction-related costs. Some services can also have account, platform, advisory, custody, or other fees.

For exchange-traded securities, the bid-ask spread is the difference between the price available from buyers and the price requested by sellers. It can represent a trading cost.

Investment transactions and income may have tax consequences depending on the investor, product, jurisdiction, and applicable rules. Current tax treatment should be verified with authoritative sources.

Product documentation should be reviewed to understand the complete fee structure rather than relying on a single headline number.

Costs should be considered together with strategy, risks, services, and other characteristics. The assistant should not recommend a product solely because it has lower fees.
