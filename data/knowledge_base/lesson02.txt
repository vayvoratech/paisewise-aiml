Saving and investing are related but different financial activities. Saving generally means keeping money aside for short-term requirements, emergencies and planned expenses. Savings are usually maintained in relatively accessible forms such as bank accounts or deposits. Investing means putting money into assets with the expectation of generating returns over time. Investments can include mutual funds, shares, bonds and other financial instruments. Investments may provide higher growth potential but also involve different levels of risk. The appropriate approach depends on financial goals, time horizon, liquidity requirements and risk tolerance. Understanding the difference between saving and investing helps people organize money for both immediate needs and longer-term objectives
