The stock market provides a platform where eligible securities can be bought and sold. Investors may participate in the market through regulated intermediaries such as brokers. Stock prices change because of supply and demand and can be influenced by company results, economic conditions, interest rates, news and investor expectations. Major Indian stock exchanges include NSE and BSE. Market indices such as NIFTY 50 and SENSEX track selected groups of companies according to defined methodologies. Understanding market terminology such as shares, market capitalization, trading volume, bid price and ask price helps beginners interpret basic market information.
