Debt investments represent lending money to an issuer in exchange for agreed terms regarding repayment and, where applicable, interest. Examples include bonds, government securities and certain money-market instruments. Debt investments can have risks including credit risk, interest rate risk and liquidity risk. When interest rates change, the market value of existing fixed-income securities can change. The level of risk depends on factors such as the issuer, maturity, interest structure and market conditions. Understanding these characteristics helps investors distinguish debt investments from equity investments and evaluate their different risk and return profiles.
