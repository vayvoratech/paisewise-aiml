A demat account is used to hold eligible securities electronically. A trading account is generally used to place buy and sell orders through a broker. These accounts serve different purposes but work together when investors transact in securities markets. Depositories such as NSDL and CDSL provide electronic securities holding infrastructure through Depository Participants. Investors should keep their account credentials secure and understand applicable charges, transaction records and statements. Basic knowledge of demat and trading accounts helps beginners understand how securities ownership and market transactions are handled electronically.
