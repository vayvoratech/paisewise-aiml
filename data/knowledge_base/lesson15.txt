NAV stands for Net Asset Value. It represents the per-unit value of a mutual fund scheme. It is generally calculated using the value of the scheme's assets after accounting for applicable liabilities and expenses, divided by the number of outstanding units, according to the applicable methodology. NAV can change as the value of securities held by the scheme changes. A lower NAV does not automatically mean that one mutual fund is cheaper or better than another. Investors should consider the scheme's objective, portfolio, performance, costs and risk characteristics rather than judging a fund solely by its NAV.
