Mutual funds can be classified according to their investment strategy and asset allocation. Equity funds primarily invest in shares, while debt funds primarily invest in fixed-income securities. Hybrid funds invest across multiple asset classes. Other categories include index funds, sectoral funds, thematic funds, liquid funds and fund-of-funds. Different categories have different objectives and risk characteristics. Investors should understand the scheme's investment objective, asset allocation, costs and associated risks before making decisions. Classification helps investors understand what a particular scheme is designed to invest in and how it may behave under different market conditions.
