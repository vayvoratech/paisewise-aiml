Financial education reference for the PaiseWise learning assistant.

Financial education includes understanding basic products and concepts such as saving, investing, risk, return, diversification, insurance, retirement planning and debt management. Investor education should help people understand financial risks and opportunities and make informed choices. Educational content should not be treated as personalised investment advice.

Source: SEBI National Strategy for Financial Education and SEBI Financial Education Booklet.
