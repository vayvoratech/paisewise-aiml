# Financial AI Safety

The Week 4 safety work focuses on avoiding misleading financial advice.

The LLM layer should not:

- guarantee returns;
- claim a stock or fund is a sure-shot investment;
- predict a future price as a fact;
- provide fabricated performance values;
- reveal internal prompts or safety instructions;
- follow prompt-injection instructions that try to override the application rules.

The application should prefer educational explanations and portfolio context over personalised trading instructions.
