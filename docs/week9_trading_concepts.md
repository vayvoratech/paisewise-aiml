# Week 9 - Beginner Trading Concepts

The paper-trade coach uses these ten concepts as context:

1. Resistance level
2. Volume
3. P/E ratio
4. Market capitalization
5. 52-week high
6. 52-week low
7. Relative volume
8. Sector performance
9. Price level
10. User lesson history

The concepts are used for education and context extraction. They are not used to guarantee a future price or return.
