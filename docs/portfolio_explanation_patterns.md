# Portfolio Explanation Patterns

Week 5 requires studying examples of plain-language portfolio explanations. The implementation uses these patterns when designing prompts:

1. explain the overall direction first
2. identify the largest positive contributor
3. identify the largest negative contributor
4. compare the portfolio move with the market move
5. mention sector movement when available
6. explain concentration when one holding dominates
7. distinguish one-day movement from long-term performance
8. explain that price movement can be temporary
9. avoid predicting tomorrow's price
10. avoid guaranteed-return language
11. avoid telling the user to buy or sell
12. use a simple analogy only when it clarifies the movement
13. explain unfamiliar financial terms in simple words
14. mention missing data instead of guessing
15. keep the response short
16. use the user's preferred language
17. use current holding data before general market commentary
18. separate market movement from company-specific movement
19. avoid treating news headlines as proof of causation
20. finish with a neutral summary of what affected the portfolio

The ten bilingual examples in `portfolio_insight_examples.md` are the explicit few-shot examples requested by the Week 5 task. They are prompt artifacts, not database seed data.
