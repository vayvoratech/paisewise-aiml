# Week 9 - Paper Trade Evaluation Rubric

A paper-trade decision is evaluated for educational quality, not future profit.

## Good decision signals

- The user has checked the current price level and relevant resistance.
- Volume is reasonably supportive compared with average volume.
- The user has considered the stock's 52-week range.
- The user understands the sector context.
- The user has studied the concepts needed to understand the decision.

## Poor decision signals

- The user enters without checking price context.
- Volume is materially below average and the user has not considered why.
- The trade is close to a known resistance level without a documented reason.
- The user has not studied the concepts needed to understand the trade.
- The decision is based only on a prediction or guaranteed-return claim.

The rubric produces a coaching score and feedback. It does not produce a real-money buy/sell instruction.
